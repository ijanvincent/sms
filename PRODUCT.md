# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the owner-operator of a self-hosted SMS gateway. They monitor queue health, confirm message delivery, register Android sender phones, and issue scoped credentials while operating the system from desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

SMS Gateway replaces per-message commercial SMS providers with an owned control plane and Android phones using carrier promo plans. Success means messages move visibly and reliably from API request to cellular send, failures are actionable, devices are easy to monitor, and sensitive credentials remain controlled.

## Brand Personality

Operational, trustworthy, and restrained. Copy should be direct and calm, with enough technical specificity to support confident decisions without turning routine monitoring into developer tooling noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, attention-seeking animation, ambiguous status colors, oversized marketing metrics, and hidden operational state. Do not make security-sensitive actions feel casual or expose raw credentials after their one-time issuance.

## Design Principles

- Put current system state ahead of decoration.
- Make freshness, failures, and next actions explicit.
- Preserve operator focus during automatic updates.
- Use one consistent vocabulary for queue, device, and credential status.
- Keep sensitive information least-privilege and intentionally revealed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Preserve semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, screen-reader announcements for meaningful live-state changes, and reduced-motion preferences.
